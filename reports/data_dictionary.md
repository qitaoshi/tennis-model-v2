## Retirements, walkovers, defaults

TML has no flag column: status is embedded in `score` as `RET`, `W/O` or `DEF`. Counts:


| outcome | rows | share | downstream policy (ground rule 6) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `completed` | 114,421 | 96.1% | Used everywhere. |
| `retired` | 3,986 | 3.3% | EXCLUDED from Stage 2 serve-rate fitting, Stage 1 validation targets, Stage 6 venue history and Stage 7 correction measurement — the stats are contaminated by whatever caused the retirement and the games distribution is truncated. INCLUDED in Stage 3 Elo as a win/loss: TML records a winner and the completed portion of the score, so the result is completed-enough for a win/loss update. Excluded from holdout outcome scoring and reported separately. |
| `walkover` | 610 | 0.5% | Never updates anything — not Elo, not rates, not venue history. No match was played; the score field is bare 'W/O'. |
